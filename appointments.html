Prepare for your appointment
What to bring
  • Available reports, scans and previous prescriptions
  • A list of current medicines and allergies

Can I reschedule?
If your plans change, you can message in the same WhatsApp conversation. Check the listed OPD hours for your next visit; for Garhwa, check the visiting date.
What if I have not received a reply?
You can attend the Ranchi OPDs during the listed hours or call 62060 91982 for help. For Garhwa, check the visiting date before travelling. For urgent medical concerns, seek in-person hospital care.
