Surgical second opinion in Ranchi

Already advised surgery, or unsure about the next step? Meet Dr. Vikeerna Bharti to discuss your reports, the recommendation you received and the questions that matter to you.

MS General Surgery, PGIMER Chandigarh · FMAS · FIAGES · FALS (Robotic)

Request a second-opinion visitCall 62060 91982

In-person visits in Bariatu or at Samford Super Speciality Hospital, Ranchi. Garhwa visits require date confirmation.

What you can discuss

Make space for your questions

  • What does my diagnosis mean?
  • Why has this operation been recommended?
  • Are there other options appropriate for me?
  • What are the risks, recovery expectations and follow-up needs?
  • What does the estimate include?

A second opinion may support the existing recommendation or identify matters that need further assessment. It does not guarantee a different treatment plan.

Bring what you already have

  • Previous prescriptions and the treatment recommendation
  • Available blood reports, scans and imaging reports
  • Previous operation notes or discharge summaries, if applicable
  • Your medicine and allergy list
  • The questions you want to discuss

You can request a visit without uploading reports or entering a medical history on this website.

How to arrange your visit

Choose a clinic. Send a request.

  1. Choose your clinic and visit purpose

    Request an OPD consultation, second opinion or follow-up.

  2. Send your request on WhatsApp

    Review the message and press Send in WhatsApp. Opening the app does not send it.

  3. Plan your OPD visit

    Visit during the listed OPD hours and bring your reports and prescriptions. For help choosing a clinic or planning your visit, call or WhatsApp. For Garhwa, check the visiting date before travelling.

Is this an online report review?

This page helps you arrange an in-person consultation. Sending a message or report does not create a diagnosis, online consultation or confirmed appointment.

Should I delay urgent treatment for a second opinion?

No. If urgent treatment has been advised, seek timely in-person care. Do not wait for a website or WhatsApp response in an emergency.

Will I need further tests?

That depends on your existing reports and assessment. Bring available records; further investigations can be discussed at the consultation.
