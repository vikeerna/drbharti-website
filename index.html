About Dr. Vikeerna Bharti

Calculate Your Body Mass Index (BMI)

BMI is a screening tool used to assess if a patient's weight falls within a healthy range. In properly evaluated patients with significant obesity (BMI > 32.5 with comorbidities like type 2 diabetes or hypertension), laparoscopic sleeve gastrectomy can support metabolic improvement.

BMI Range Classification Surgical Indications
Below 18.5 Underweight Not indicated
18.5 – 24.9 Normal Weight Not indicated
25.0 – 29.9 Overweight Lifestyle & Diet management
30.0 – 34.9 Obesity Class I Eligible if comorbidities exist
35.0 and above Class II & III Evaluated for Bariatric Surgery

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about surgical procedures, recovery timelines, and clinic consultations in Ranchi.

Where can I consult Dr. Vikeerna Bharti in Ranchi?+

Consult Dr. Vikeerna Bharti at the Bariatu clinic near Manipal Hospital or at Samford Super Speciality Hospital, Kokar Chowk, Ranchi. Call 62060 91982 to confirm the clinic and appointment time before travelling.

Stomach pain after eating fatty food — what is it?+

Severe pain in the upper right side of your stomach that triggers or worsens after consuming oily or heavy food is a classic sign of gallstones. The gallbladder contracts to release digestive bile, pressing against the stones. If you have these symptoms, you should get an abdominal ultrasound evaluation. Call +916206091982.

Lump in groin when standing — is it hernia?+

Yes, a lump or swelling in the groin or abdomen that becomes prominent when standing, straining, or coughing, and disappears when lying flat, is typically a hernia. Since hernias are physical wall defects, they do not heal with medicines and require surgical repair (laparoscopic TEP/TAPP/eTEP or open mesh repair) to avoid risk of strangulation.

Bleeding during bowel movements — what to do?+

Bright red bleeding or sharp pain during motion is most commonly caused by piles (haemorrhoids) or an anal fissure. Bleeding from the anal region should never be self-treated or ignored. Dr. Vikeerna Bharti provides expert clinical evaluation and definitive treatment (minimally invasive surgical procedures) at Samford Hospital, Ranchi.

How long is recovery after laparoscopic hernia or gallbladder surgery?+

Recovery depends on the case and procedure. Most planned laparoscopic gallbladder removals and hernia repairs allow for next-day discharge from the hospital. Patients can usually walk on the same evening, resume light home/office activities in 5 to 7 days, and return to full active life in 3 to 4 weeks.

Is bariatric surgery safe and available in Ranchi?+

Yes. Bariatric surgery is available at Samford Hospital, Ranchi, under Dr. Vikeerna Bharti. Laparoscopic sleeve gastrectomy is performed for clinically selected, properly evaluated patients. Metabolic improvement may occur post-operation. It is a highly regulated procedure requiring long-term follow-up and lifestyle commitment.

Swollen twisted veins on legs — treatment in Ranchi?+

Bulging, twisted veins in the lower legs are varicose veins. Dr. Vikeerna Bharti offers Radiofrequency Ablation (RFA) at Samford Hospital, Ranchi. This is a day-care procedure in selected patients performed under local anaesthesia with minimal or no sutures, allowing patients to go home shortly after the procedure.

Pain right side abdomen with fever — appendicitis?+

Acute pain starting around the navel and shifting to the lower right abdomen, accompanied by fever, nausea, or vomiting, indicates appendicitis. This is a general surgical emergency. You should consult a surgeon immediately. Emergency laparoscopic appendicectomy is available on-call at Samford Hospital, Ranchi.
