He manages symptomatic gallstones, acute cholecystitis and recurrent gallbladder pain using minimally invasive laparoscopic cholecystectomy whenever appropriate.

What is the Gallbladder?

The gallbladder stores bile — a digestive juice that breaks down fats. When bile becomes too concentrated, hard stones form inside (cholelithiasis). These cause pain, nausea, and can lead to serious complications if untreated.

Symptoms
  • Severe pain in upper right abdomen, may radiate to the back or shoulder
  • Pain especially after eating fatty or fried foods
  • Nausea, vomiting, and bloating after meals
  • Jaundice (yellowing of skin/eyes) if stone blocks the bile duct
  • Many patients have no symptoms — but untreated stones cause serious complications
Complications if Untreated
  • Cholecystitis — severe infection/inflammation of the gallbladder
  • Biliary pancreatitis — life-threatening inflammation of the pancreas
  • Cholangitis — dangerous bile duct infection causing jaundice and sepsis
  • Gallbladder cancer — over 85% of gallbladder cancers are linked to untreated stones
Treatment — Laparoscopic Cholecystectomy in Ranchi

Surgery is the only permanent cure. Dr. Bharti performs Laparoscopic Cholecystectomy — a keyhole procedure using 3–4 tiny incisions. The gallbladder is safely removed through a small cut. Most patients are discharged the same day or next morning with minimal pain.

What is a Hernia?

A hernia occurs when an internal organ pushes through a weakness in the surrounding muscle wall, creating a visible bulge. Hernias never heal on their own. Untreated hernias can become strangulated — cutting off blood supply — which is a surgical emergency.

Types of Hernia Treated
  • Inguinal Hernia — Groin bulge; most common, especially in men
  • Umbilical / Para-umbilical — Around the belly button
  • Incisional Hernia — At the site of a previous surgery scar
  • Ventral / Epigastric — Abdominal wall, upper central abdomen
  • Femoral Hernia — Upper thigh, just below the groin
Advanced Techniques Available

For large, complex, or recurrent hernias, Dr. Bharti performs Abdominal Wall Reconstruction (AWR), Component Separation, and Transversus Abdominis Release (TAR) — expertise available at very few centres in Jharkhand.

Piles — Open Haemorrhoidectomy

Piles are swollen veins in and around the anus. Extremely common — yet many patients delay treatment. Symptoms include bright red bleeding during bowel movements, pain, and itching. Dr. Bharti performs Open Haemorrhoidectomy — a definitive surgical procedure that permanently removes haemorrhoidal tissue, offering a lasting cure for symptomatic piles.

Anal Fistula — Fistulectomy

An anal fistula is an abnormal tunnel from the anal canal to the skin around the anus, causing persistent discharge and repeated infections. It cannot heal without surgery. Dr. Bharti performs Fistulectomy — complete surgical excision of the fistula tract — with careful sphincter preservation and focus on long-term cure.

Anal Fissure — Lateral Internal Sphincterotomy

Chronic anal fissures causing severe pain during bowel movements are treated with Lateral Internal Sphincterotomy (LIS) — a targeted procedure that relieves anal spasm and allows permanent healing.

Rectal Prolapse Surgery in Jharkhand

Rectal prolapse often causes embarrassment and delayed consultation, but early treatment gives better functional recovery. Dr. Vikeerna Bharti offers evaluation for rectal prolapse in Ranchi and performs laparoscopic rectopexy for suitable patients needing durable correction with faster recovery than traditional open surgery.

What is Rectal Prolapse?

Rectal prolapse occurs when the rectum slides out of position and protrudes through the anus. It causes significant discomfort, faecal incontinence, mucus discharge, and difficulty with bowel function. It worsens progressively without surgical treatment.

Symptoms
  • A reddish mass protruding through the anus on straining
  • Difficulty controlling bowel movements (faecal incontinence)
  • Feeling of incomplete bowel emptying
  • Mucus or blood discharge from the anus
Treatment — Laparoscopic Rectopexy

Dr. Vikeerna Bharti performs Laparoscopic Rectopexy — the gold-standard, minimally invasive procedure for rectal prolapse. The rectum is carefully mobilised and fixed to the sacrum using sutures or mesh, preventing recurrence. Advantages include faster recovery, less pain, 2–3 day hospital stay, and excellent long-term results with very low recurrence rates.
