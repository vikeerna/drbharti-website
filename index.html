Before your visit
A few practical questions
For appointment help, call 62060 91982.
Where can I consult Dr. Vikeerna Bharti in Ranchi?
At Ranchi Dental and Polyclinic, Bariatu (Mon–Sat 9–10 AM and 5–7:30 PM), and at Samford Super Speciality Hospital, Kokar Chowk (Mon–Sat 11 AM–5 PM). You can attend during OPD hours without waiting for a WhatsApp reply. Call 62060 91982.
Stomach pain after eating fatty food — what is it?
Pain in the upper right abdomen after oily food is a common symptom of gallbladder stones. Other conditions can feel similar, so an ultrasound and examination are needed.
Lump in groin when standing — is it a hernia?
A groin swelling that appears on standing or coughing and goes back on lying down is often a hernia. Hernias do not heal with medicines; a surgeon will examine you and discuss repair.
Bleeding during bowel movements — what to do?
Bright red bleeding is often due to piles or a fissure, but it should not be ignored or self-treated for long. See a surgeon for examination.
How long is recovery after laparoscopic hernia or gallbladder surgery?
After planned keyhole surgery, most patients go home the next day, walk the same evening, return to light work in 5–7 days and to full activity in 3–4 weeks. Recovery varies with the operation and your health.
Is bariatric surgery available in Ranchi?
Yes. Dr. Bharti performs laparoscopic sleeve gastrectomy at Samford Super Speciality Hospital for carefully assessed patients, with long-term follow-up.
Swollen, twisted veins on legs — treatment in Ranchi?
These are usually varicose veins. After a Doppler scan, Dr. Bharti performs radiofrequency ablation (RFA) at Samford Super Speciality Hospital; patients usually go home the next day.
Pain in the right side of the abdomen with fever — appendicitis?
Pain moving to the lower right abdomen with fever or vomiting may be appendicitis, a surgical emergency. Go to a hospital emergency the same day. Samford Super Speciality Hospital has 24/7 emergency surgery.
Is there a surgeon available in Garhwa, Jharkhand?
Yes. Dr. Bharti usually visits Sai Sushila Sishu Hospital, Garhwa, on the first and third Sundays, where general and laparoscopic surgery, including hernia surgery, is available. Call hospital reception on 82641 03707 to check the date before travelling.
Where can I consult a surgeon in Bariatu, Ranchi in the morning or evening?
At Ranchi Dental and Polyclinic, Bariatu Road, near Manipal Hospital: Monday to Saturday, 9–10 AM and 5–7:30 PM. Sunday closed.
What should I bring?
Bring available reports and scans, previous prescriptions, a list of medicines and allergies, and any previous operation or discharge records. You do not need to order new tests just to request an appointment.
Can I come for a second opinion?
Yes. Bring the existing treatment recommendation and available reports so that your concerns and options can be discussed in person.
Do I need a reply before visiting?
You can attend the Ranchi OPDs during the listed hours without waiting for a WhatsApp confirmation. For help with timing, call 62060 91982. Garhwa visits depend on the visiting schedule, so check the date before travelling.
Where is surgery done if it is advised?
Planned and emergency operations are done at Samford Super Speciality Hospital, Ranchi. General and laparoscopic surgery, including hernia surgery, is also available at Sai Sushila Sishu Hospital, Garhwa. The right hospital is discussed after assessment.
Is cashless treatment or Ayushman Bharat available?
At Samford Super Speciality Hospital, where Dr. Bharti operates, cashless treatment is available under major health-insurance and TPA plans, Ayushman Bharat and the Jharkhand Rajya Karmi Swasthya Bima Yojana (state employees’ health insurance).
