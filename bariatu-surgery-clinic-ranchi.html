Frequently asked questions

Can I visit without an appointment?

Yes. You can attend Ranchi Dental and Polyclinic during the OPD hours, Monday to Saturday 9–10 AM and 5–7:30 PM, without waiting for a WhatsApp confirmation.

Where is the clinic?

Ranchi Dental and Polyclinic is on Bariatu Road, near Manipal Hospital, Ranchi. Use the directions link to open it in Google Maps.
