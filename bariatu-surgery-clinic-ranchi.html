Visit the Bariatu clinic

Location: Bariatu Road, near Manipal Hospital, Ranchi, Jharkhand 834009.

Usual consultation hours: Monday to Saturday, 9–10 AM and 5–8 PM. Sunday closed. Please call or WhatsApp to confirm the time before travelling, as hospital commitments can affect availability.

Open the Bariatu clinic on Google Maps →

What you can consult for

The clinic offers general surgical assessment, discussion of treatment options and follow-up. A consultation does not automatically mean that an operation is needed.

  • Gallbladder stones and related surgical concerns
  • Hernia and abdominal wall concerns
  • Varicose veins and treatment assessment
  • Piles, fissure and fistula
  • Thyroid swelling, breast concerns, lumps and other general surgical problems
  • Review after surgery and discussion of existing investigation reports

Before your appointment

  1. Call or WhatsApp 62060 91982 and specify Bariatu.
  2. Confirm the date, time and consultation fee. Sending a WhatsApp message alone does not confirm a booking.
  3. Bring relevant reports, previous prescriptions or discharge papers, and your current medication list.

WhatsApp opens with a booking message; you choose whether to send it. Replies can be delayed during surgery. For an emergency, seek urgent in-person hospital care rather than waiting for a reply.

If hospital treatment is advised

Dr. Bharti also practises at Samford Super Speciality Hospital, Kokar, Ranchi. The appropriate facility and next steps are discussed after assessment. Any insurance or scheme eligibility must be confirmed with the hospital for the individual case.

For visits to Sai Sushila Sishu Hospital, see the separate Garhwa information page and confirm Dr. Bharti's attendance before travelling.
