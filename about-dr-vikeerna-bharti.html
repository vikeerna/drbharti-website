About Dr. Vikeerna Bharti

Consultant general and laparoscopic surgeon in Ranchi, Jharkhand.

MBBS · MS General Surgery (PGIMER Chandigarh) · FMAS · FIAGES · FALS (Robotic)

Dr. Vikeerna Bharti is a consultant general and laparoscopic surgeon in Ranchi, Jharkhand. He holds an MS in General Surgery from PGIMER Chandigarh, completed senior residency at PGIMER and AIIMS Bhubaneswar, and now practises at Samford Super Speciality Hospital, Ranchi, with a morning and evening OPD at Ranchi Dental and Polyclinic, Bariatu, and a visiting OPD at Sai Sushila Sishu Hospital, Garhwa.

His practice covers advanced laparoscopic and gastrointestinal surgery, emergency and trauma surgery, abdominal wall reconstruction, bariatric and metabolic surgery, breast and endocrine surgery, and vein treatment including radiofrequency ablation.

Operative experience

From Dr. Bharti’s September 2026 CV. The right operation for an individual patient is decided after assessment.

Elective laparoscopic and GI surgery

  • Laparoscopic cholecystectomy, including difficult and frozen Calot’s triangle, empyema and Mirizzi syndrome
  • Laparoscopic appendicectomy and splenectomy
  • Laparoscopic hemicolectomy and bowel resection
  • Laparoscopic management of hydatid cyst of the liver
  • Common bile duct exploration for bile-duct stones

Hernia and abdominal wall reconstruction

  • Inguinal hernia repair — TEP, TAPP and eTEP
  • Ventral, incisional and complex abdominal-wall hernia repair — IPOM, Rives–Stoppa, eTEP-RS and TAR
  • Femoral, recurrent and primary lumbar hernia repair

Emergency and trauma surgery

  • Perforation peritonitis, appendicular abscess and lump
  • Intestinal obstruction and obstructed or strangulated hernia
  • Emergency laparotomy, bowel resection and stoma
  • Abdominal and chest trauma, damage-control surgery and vascular repair

Foregut, hiatal and pelvic floor surgery

  • Laparoscopic fundoplication for GERD; hiatal and paraoesophageal hernia repair
  • Heller cardiomyotomy for achalasia cardia
  • Laparoscopic rectopexy for rectal prolapse

Bariatric and metabolic surgery

  • Laparoscopic sleeve gastrectomy as primary surgeon
  • Established the obesity and metabolic clinic at Samford Super Speciality Hospital

Vascular, breast, endocrine and proctology

  • Radiofrequency ablation and surgery for varicose veins
  • Breast lumps, breast-conservation surgery and modified radical mastectomy
  • Hemithyroidectomy, total thyroidectomy and superficial parotidectomy
  • Piles, fistula-in-ano, fissure and pilonidal sinus

Surgical oncology

  • Radical cholecystectomy for gallbladder cancer
  • Right and left hemicolectomy, anterior resection and abdominoperineal resection for colorectal cancer
  • Wide local excision of soft-tissue and skin cancers

Thoracic and thoracoscopic surgery

  • Video-assisted thoracoscopic surgery (VATS) for empyema
  • Thoracoscopic surgery for lung hydatid cyst and adhesions
  • Thoracotomy, chest drain insertion and management of chest trauma
