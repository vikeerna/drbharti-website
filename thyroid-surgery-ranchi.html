What is Thyroid Surgery?

The thyroid gland is a butterfly-shaped gland in the front of the neck that produces hormones controlling metabolism, energy and body temperature. Surgery is required when the thyroid develops conditions that cannot be managed with medication alone — including goitre, thyroid nodules, hyperthyroidism or thyroid cancer.

Dr. Vikeerna Bharti performs thyroid surgery at Samford Super Speciality Hospital, Kokar Chowk, Ranchi, bringing expert-level thyroid surgery to Jharkhand and eliminating the need for patients to travel to Delhi or Chandigarh.

Conditions Requiring Thyroid Surgery

  • Goitre (Enlarged Thyroid) — Large goitre causing difficulty swallowing, breathing or cosmetic concern
  • Thyroid Nodule — Solid or suspicious nodule detected on ultrasound requiring FNAC and possibly removal
  • Thyroid Cancer — Papillary, follicular, medullary or anaplastic thyroid cancer
  • Hyperthyroidism — Overactive thyroid not responding to radioiodine or medication (Graves disease)
  • Multinodular Goitre — Multiple nodules in the thyroid causing enlargement

Symptoms That Warrant Consultation

  • Visible or palpable swelling in the front of the neck
  • Difficulty swallowing (dysphagia) — food or liquid
  • Hoarseness of voice or change in voice quality
  • Difficulty breathing, especially when lying flat
  • Thyroid nodule detected on ultrasound or CT scan
  • Rapidly growing neck swelling

Types of Thyroid Surgery

  • Total Thyroidectomy — Complete removal of the thyroid; used for thyroid cancer and large bilateral goitres
  • Hemithyroidectomy (Lobectomy) — Removal of one lobe; for single nodule or one-sided disease
  • Near-Total Thyroidectomy — Removal of most of the thyroid with a small remnant preserved

Recovery After Thyroid Surgery

  • Hospital stay: 1–2 days
  • Return to normal activities: 1–2 weeks
  • Voice usually returns to normal within days
  • Thyroid hormone (thyroxine) tablets required if total thyroidectomy performed

I have a swelling in my neck — do I need surgery?+

A swelling in the front of the neck is most commonly a goitre or thyroid nodule. Not all require surgery. Dr. Vikeerna Bharti evaluates with clinical examination, ultrasound and FNAC (biopsy if needed). Surgery is recommended for large, growing, suspicious or cancerous nodules. Consult at Samford Hospital, Ranchi — call +916206091982.

Is thyroid surgery safe in Ranchi?+

Yes. Thyroidectomy is a safe, well-established procedure when performed by an experienced surgeon. Dr. Vikeerna Bharti performs thyroid surgery at Samford Super Speciality Hospital, Ranchi, with careful attention to protecting the parathyroid glands and recurrent laryngeal nerve (voice nerve).

Will I need thyroid tablets after surgery?+

If total thyroidectomy (full removal) is performed, lifelong thyroid hormone replacement (thyroxine) is required. This is a simple once-daily tablet. After hemithyroidectomy (one lobe removed), about half of patients do not need thyroid tablets.

What is the recovery time after thyroid surgery?+

Most patients are discharged within 1–2 days after thyroid surgery. Return to normal activities in 1–2 weeks. Voice may be slightly hoarse for a few days but usually returns to normal quickly.

Does Dr. Bharti treat thyroid cancer in Ranchi?+

Yes. Dr. Vikeerna Bharti performs thyroidectomy for thyroid cancer at Samford Hospital, Ranchi. Early-stage thyroid cancer (papillary and follicular types) has an excellent prognosis with surgery. Early consultation is strongly recommended for any suspicious thyroid nodule.
