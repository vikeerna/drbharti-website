Piles (Haemorrhoids) — Treatment in Ranchi

Piles are swollen blood vessels in and around the anus. Extremely common — yet many patients delay seeking help due to embarrassment. Bleeding during bowel movements is the most common symptom. Left untreated, piles enlarge progressively and become increasingly difficult to manage.

Symptoms of Piles

  • Bright red bleeding during or after passing stools — on tissue paper or in the toilet
  • Pain, discomfort or burning around the anus
  • Itching around the anus
  • A lump protruding from the anus (prolapsed piles) — may need to be pushed back
  • Feeling of incomplete bowel emptying

Treatment — Open Haemorrhoidectomy

Dr. Vikeerna Bharti performs Open Haemorrhoidectomy - the definitive surgical removal of haemorrhoidal tissue. This procedure offers durable surgical relief in properly selected patients for grade 3 and grade 4 piles and for patients who have failed other treatments. Recovery takes 2-4 weeks with appropriate wound care.

Anal Fistula — Fistulectomy in Ranchi

An anal fistula is an abnormal tunnel connecting the anal canal to the skin around the anus. It causes persistent discharge, itching and repeated infections. It cannot heal without surgery. Dr. Bharti performs Fistulectomy — complete surgical excision of the fistula tract — with careful sphincter preservation to prevent incontinence.

Anal Fissure — LIS (Lateral Internal Sphincterotomy)

A chronic anal fissure causes severe tearing pain during and after bowel movements, often accompanied by bleeding. It is caused by a tight anal sphincter that prevents healing. Dr. Bharti performs Lateral Internal Sphincterotomy (LIS) - a targeted procedure that relieves the muscle spasm, allowing the fissure to heal and providing long-term relief.

Bleeding during bowel movements - what to do?+

Bright red bleeding during or after passing stools is the classic symptom of piles (haemorrhoids). It should not be ignored as it worsens over time. Dr. Vikeerna Bharti provides confidential consultation and surgical treatment at Samford Hospital, Kokar Chowk, Ranchi. Call +916206091982.

Is piles surgery painful?+

Modern haemorrhoidectomy is performed under anaesthesia, so there is no pain during the procedure. Post-operative pain is managed effectively with medications. Most planned cases are discharged the next day and are back to normal activities within 2–3 weeks.

Can fistula heal without surgery?+

No. Anal fistula is an abnormal channel that cannot heal spontaneously. Without surgery, it causes repeated infections and abscesses. Fistulectomy — complete excision of the fistula tract — is the only definitive treatment. Dr. Bharti performs this at Samford Hospital, Ranchi.

What is the difference between piles and fistula?+

Piles are swollen blood vessels causing bleeding and discomfort. Fistula is an abnormal tunnel from inside the anal canal to the skin outside, causing persistent discharge. Both are different conditions requiring different surgical treatment. Dr. Bharti treats both at Samford Hospital, Ranchi.

How long is recovery after piles surgery?+

Most planned cases are discharged the next day. Pain is manageable with medication. Return to desk work in 1–2 weeks. Full recovery in 3–4 weeks. Fibre-rich diet and adequate water intake speeds recovery significantly.

Which doctor should I see for piles, fissure or fistula in Ranchi?+

Piles, fissure and fistula are managed by a general surgeon. Many early cases settle with diet, medication and office procedures, so an operation is not always the first step.
