Surgical care in Garhwa, without travelling to Ranchi

For many families in Garhwa district, an operation used to mean a full day's journey to Ranchi or Varanasi, an attendant's time off work and travel costs, often for a procedure that takes about an hour.

Sai Sushila Sishu Hospital now has a modular operation theatre with laparoscopy, and Dr. Vikeerna Bharti — MS General Surgery, PGIMER Chandigarh; former senior resident, AIIMS Bhubaneswar; FMAS, FIAGES, FALS (Robotic) — holds a surgical OPD here on the usual first and third Sundays. General surgery and laparoscopic surgery, including hernia surgery, are available here for patients assessed as suitable, close to home.

Suitability for surgery in Garhwa is decided at the consultation. Complex cases, or patients who may need intensive care after an operation, are advised surgery at Samford Super Speciality Hospital, Ranchi, where Dr. Bharti also operates. This is discussed openly with you and your family.

Surgical facilities at Garhwa

Modular operation theatre with Stryker laparoscopy

A modular OT with a Stryker laparoscopy (दूरबीन) system for keyhole surgery, set up to the standard-of-care requirements used for planned surgery.

GE anaesthesia workstation

Anaesthesia delivery and patient monitoring during the operation.

Horizontal autoclave sterilisation

Steam sterilisation of surgical instruments for every operation.

The hospital's suitability for each operation is assessed before surgery is planned.

Frequently asked questions

When does Dr. Vikeerna Bharti visit Garhwa?

Dr. Bharti usually holds a surgical OPD at Sai Sushila Sishu Hospital on the first and third Sundays of the month. Dates can change because of clinical commitments, so call hospital reception on 82641 03707 to check the date before travelling.

Is there a facility for operations in Garhwa, or do I have to go to Ranchi?

Sai Sushila Sishu Hospital has a modular operation theatre with a Stryker laparoscopy system, a GE anaesthesia workstation and horizontal autoclave sterilisation, so many planned operations can be done in Garhwa without travelling to Ranchi. Complex cases, or patients who may need intensive care after surgery, are advised surgery at Samford Super Speciality Hospital, Ranchi. This is decided after examination.

Which surgeries are available in Garhwa?

General surgery and laparoscopic (keyhole) surgery, including hernia surgery, are available at Sai Sushila Sishu Hospital for patients assessed as suitable. Suitability for surgery in Garhwa is discussed at the consultation.

How do I check the next OPD date?

Call Sai Sushila Sishu Hospital reception on 82641 03707 a few days before travelling. You can also message Dr. Bharti on WhatsApp. The date shown on this website is calculated from the usual schedule and is not live availability.

What should I bring to the Garhwa OPD?

Bring ultrasound or other scan reports, recent blood tests, previous prescriptions or discharge papers and your current medicine list.

How is treatment paid for in Garhwa?

Consultation and surgery at Sai Sushila Sishu Hospital are self-paid; ask the hospital for an estimate. Cashless insurance, TPA, Ayushman Bharat and Rajya Karmi Swasthya Bima Yojana treatment is available at Samford Super Speciality Hospital, Ranchi.

गढ़वा में सर्जन कब बैठते हैं?
