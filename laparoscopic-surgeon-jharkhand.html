Coming to Ranchi from another district

Patients travel for consultation and surgery from Hazaribagh, Ramgarh, Khunti, Gumla, Lohardaga, Latehar, Palamu (Daltonganj), Garhwa, Chatra, Simdega, Bokaro and nearby districts.

  • Before you travel: check the OPD hours and directions; for Garhwa, call the hospital to check the date.
  • Bring: all reports and scan images (ultrasound, CT or Doppler), blood tests, prescriptions, discharge papers, your medicine list and any insurance or Ayushman card.
  • On the day: after examination, further tests may be advised. A treatment plan depends on the findings; it cannot be fixed from an enquiry alone.
  • If surgery is planned: you will be told about admission, what to bring and the expected stay. Insurance and Ayushman Bharat: at Samford Super Speciality Hospital, where Dr. Bharti operates, cashless treatment is available under major health-insurance and TPA plans, Ayushman Bharat and the Jharkhand Rajya Karmi Swasthya Bima Yojana (state employees’ health insurance). Eligibility depends on your policy or card and is confirmed by the hospital insurance desk.

For urgent symptoms, seek care at the nearest hospital first.

Frequently asked questions

I live outside Ranchi. How should I plan my visit?

Choose the OPD that suits your journey, bring all existing reports and scan images, and allow time in case tests are needed before a treatment plan can be made.

Where does Dr. Bharti consult?

Ranchi Dental and Polyclinic, Bariatu (Mon–Sat 9–10 AM and 5–7:30 PM); Samford Super Speciality Hospital, Kokar (Mon–Sat 11 AM–5 PM); and visiting OPD at Sai Sushila Sishu Hospital, Garhwa, usually on the first and third Sundays — check the date before travelling.

Is insurance or Ayushman Bharat accepted?

At Samford Super Speciality Hospital, cashless treatment is available under major health-insurance and TPA plans, Ayushman Bharat and the Jharkhand Rajya Karmi Swasthya Bima Yojana (state employees’ health insurance). The hospital insurance desk confirms eligibility for your policy or card.

Should I come with an attendant?

If an operation may be planned, it helps to come with a family member or attendant who can stay with you during admission.

Plan an in-person consultation

Bring your available reports, prescriptions and questions. You can attend the Ranchi OPDs during the listed hours. For Garhwa, check the visiting date before travelling.
