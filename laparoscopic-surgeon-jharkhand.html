Advanced Laparoscopic Surgery for All of Jharkhand

Dr. Vikeerna Bharti is a Consultant General, Advanced Laparoscopic & Bariatric Surgeon based at Samford Super Speciality Hospital, Kokar Chowk, Ranchi — the capital and medical hub of Jharkhand. Patients travel to his OPD from Hazaribagh, Ramgarh, Khunti, Gumla, Lohardaga, Latehar, Palamu (Daltonganj), Garhwa, Chatra, Simdega, Bokaro and nearby districts for keyhole (दूरबीन) surgery that combines metro-level technique with the convenience of treatment close to home.

He completed his MS General Surgery at PGIMER Chandigarh, served as Senior Resident Surgeon at AIIMS Bhubaneswar, and holds FMAS and FIAGES fellowships in minimal access surgery, with peer-reviewed research published in laparoscopic hernia repair.

Surgeries Available

  • Gallbladder stone surgery — laparoscopic cholecystectomy (पित्त की थैली की पथरी का दूरबीन ऑपरेशन)

  • Piles, fissure & fistula — बवासीर, फिशर, भगंदर का इलाज
  • Appendix surgery — including emergency laparoscopic appendicectomy

  • Thyroid surgery and acid reflux surgery, breast and body lumps, hydrocele

Coming From Outside Ranchi? Plan Your Visit

Most outstation patients complete consultation, report review and surgical planning in a single visit. To make your trip smooth:

  • WhatsApp your reports first to +91 62060 91982 — ultrasound/Doppler, blood tests, previous prescriptions.
  • Bring originals on the day: reports, medication list, previous discharge papers, and insurance/cashless card if applicable.
  • Insurance & cashless: Samford Hospital's insurance desk assists with pre-authorisation for eligible policies after clinical evaluation.
  • Planned surgery: for laparoscopic procedures, most patients are discharged the next day where clinically appropriate, so an attendant and one night's stay are usually sufficient.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which laparoscopic surgeries are available in Jharkhand with Dr. Vikeerna Bharti?+

Dr. Vikeerna Bharti performs laparoscopic gallbladder stone surgery (cholecystectomy), hernia repair (TEP, TAPP, eTEP), appendix surgery, piles/fissure/fistula procedures, varicose veins RFA and bariatric surgery in properly evaluated patients — at Samford Super Speciality Hospital, Kokar Chowk, Ranchi, Jharkhand.

Patients from across Jharkhand — Hazaribagh, Ramgarh, Khunti, Gumla, Lohardaga, Latehar, Palamu/Daltonganj, Bokaro, Chatra, Simdega and nearby districts — can WhatsApp their reports to +916206091982 before travelling. Where does Dr. Bharti consult in Jharkhand?+

Three locations: Samford Super Speciality Hospital, Kokar Chowk, Ranchi (Mon–Sat 11 AM–5 PM, surgery and emergencies); Bariatu OPD, Bariatu Road near Manipal Hospital (Mon–Sat 9–10 AM and 5–8 PM, consultation and follow-up); and Sai Sushila Sishu Hospital, Garhwa (1st and 3rd Sunday).

What should I bring to the first consultation?+

Bring your ultrasound or Doppler report, recent blood tests, any previous prescriptions or discharge papers, current medication list, and your insurance/cashless card if applicable. If reports are older than 3 months, fresh investigations may be advised.

Is insurance or cashless facility available for surgery in Ranchi?+

Samford Super Speciality Hospital supports insurance and cashless admission for eligible policies. Approval depends on your insurer and policy terms; the hospital insurance desk assists with pre-authorisation after clinical evaluation.
